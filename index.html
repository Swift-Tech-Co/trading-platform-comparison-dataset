Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best platform for algorithmic trading?

By use case: MetaTrader 5 (MQL5) for retail forex and CFD, cTrader (cAlgo/C#) for ECN brokers, Interactive Brokers API (Python, Java, C++) for multi-asset institutional, Alpaca (REST, Python) for US equities, dYdX for decentralized derivatives. For HFT, custom C++ with co-located FIX protocol connectivity is the only viable option.

How much does a custom trading platform cost?

Custom trading platform costs: retail forex with MT5 white label ($15,000 to $50,000 for white label plus $15,000 to $40,000 customization), fully custom platform ($150,000 to $400,000), institutional-grade with FIX and co-location ($300,000 to $1,000,000 plus).
